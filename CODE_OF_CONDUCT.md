# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes demonstrating empathy, respecting differing viewpoints, giving and
accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility for mistakes, and focusing on what is best
for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting comments,
harassment, publishing private information without permission, and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report abusive,
harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's
privacy and safety.
